01 / THE ORDINARY

CHAPTER ONE — 02:17 AM

Some nights,
the city feels
too quiet.

By day, he was just another face in the crowd—late trains, unfinished coffee, promises made to people he loved. By night, he watched the rooftops and listened for trouble.

Then every screen in the city blinked at once. A red signal pulsed above the skyline, where no signal should exist.

02 / THE FIRST SWING

CHAPTER TWO — THE UNKNOWN

Trust the leap.
Question the sky.

He fired a web into the dark and launched himself between the towers. The signal grew louder with every swing, like a heartbeat coming from somewhere beyond the clouds.

At the edge of the river, the sky split open—not with thunder, but with a silence that swallowed the city.

03 / THE FRACTURE

CHAPTER THREE — TWO CITIES

One skyline.
Two realities.

Beyond the fracture was another New York: familiar streets, unfamiliar stars. In its windows, he saw lives that almost matched his own.

A voice crackled through the rift: “If you close it, our world disappears. If you leave it open, yours may be next.”

04 / THE CHOICE

CHAPTER FOUR — NO EASY ANSWER

Being a hero
means choosing
the hard way.

He could run. He could choose the world he knew. Instead, he reached through the fracture and caught the hand of someone on the other side.

For one breath, two cities held still. Then the web between them began to glow.

CHAPTER FIVE — BEYOND THE WEB

The city still
needs a hero.

He pulled—not to destroy the bridge, but to anchor it. The worlds steadied. The signal faded. And somewhere above the skyline, a new thread caught the light.
